Privacy Policy

Manifestiq — Inspect Your Android Project Before It Ships
Last updated: 6 October 2026

Summary: Manifestiq has no servers, no accounts and no internet permission. Nothing you paste into the app is ever sent anywhere.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes how the Manifestiq Android application ("Manifestiq", "the app", "we") handles information when you use it. Manifestiq is a fully offline tool: it inspects Android project configuration text that you provide, entirely on your device.

2. Information we do not collect

Manifestiq does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. In particular, the app:

  • does not request or use the INTERNET permission — it has no network access at all;
  • contains no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking components;
  • has no sign-up, login or user account;
  • does not upload, back up or sync your data to any server.

3. Information that stays on your device

When you analyse a project, the text you provide (for example an AndroidManifest.xml or build.gradle file) is read in memory for the duration of the analysis only. The raw text is never stored.

If you keep a result, the app stores a local record in its on-device history database containing the file names, the findings, their evidence and a health score. Text that looks like a credential is masked before it is stored, so passwords and tokens are not persisted. Scan history stays on your device and you can delete individual entries or the entire history at any time from the History and Settings screens.

4. Permissions the app uses

Manifestiq requests no Android permissions. It reads the content you explicitly share with it or paste into it and nothing else. It does not read your files, contacts, location, media or any other device data on its own.

5. How configuration text is processed

Pasted text is treated as untrusted input: it is parsed, never executed. Manifestiq does not run Gradle, does not execute scripts or code from your project, and does not evaluate expressions found in the text.

6. Children's privacy

Manifestiq does not collect any information from anyone, including children, and is not directed at children as defined by applicable child-protection laws.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains functionality that processes information differently, this policy will be updated before or at the same time as that change ships, with the "Last updated" date revised accordingly. Questions about a change can be sent to the contact address below.
